# Every feature, plan by plan (v2)

Generated from `backend/app/plans.py` (the defaults). The admin can move any feature to another plan, switch it off, rename it or
change its description in **Admin → Features & plans**; the menu, the locked pages and the Plans page follow those changes at once.
Each plan includes everything in the plans before it.

**Rules that hold in every plan:** the hub's own WhatsApp number only messages owners; below Growth Mentorship every customer message
is prepared in the Send list and sent by the owner with one tap from their own WhatsApp; from Growth Mentorship it can go from the
owner's own connected number; money messages (reminders, quotations) always wait for a person; only LegacyWorkforce staff may act on
their own, never with money or links. Below ₹1.5L owners get AI tools; AI staff start at Growth Mentorship.
