# Business AI Action Hub — v2 final plan (agreed 8 Oct 2026)

Agreed with Harsh in chat on 8 Oct 2026. This replaces the earlier plan-by-plan feature list.

## Rules that apply to every plan

- **Websites live at name.employz.ai.** One wildcard record on employz.ai covers every owner, and SSL is automatic. Owners do no DNS work. A business's own domain (yourname.com) is a done-for-you step in ₹1.5L, handled with Employz.ai.
- **WhatsApp to customers.**
  - Anything sent to customers automatically (auto-reply, two-way AI chat, reminders or follow-ups that go out on their own) is **₹1.5L and ₹6L only**. It goes through AiSensy on the owner's own number.
  - Below ₹1.5L, the hub writes the message and the owner taps **Send**: their own WhatsApp opens with the message typed.
- **Owner alerts** (login code, new inquiry, "your week is ready", digest, CEO report) come from the central Employz.ai WhatsApp number. They go to the owner only, never to customers.
- **AI tools vs AI staff.** Below ₹1.5L, owners use AI *tools*. "AI staff" who work on their own start at ₹1.5L.
- **Admin controls every feature.** The admin decides:
  - which plan each feature is in (drag), on/off globally, names and descriptions, and menu order;
  - whether a locked feature shows as a teaser or is hidden;
  - each plan's price, AI runs, team size and duration;
  - bonus features for a single owner, with an end date.

  Every change is recorded in a change log with undo.

## Free — workshop: "You're online today"

- **Onboarding from the owner's website.** The hub reads the site and fills the Business Brain: name, what they sell, who for, city, phone, services, tone, logo and brand colour. The owner checks one screen. Owners without a website do a 60-second voice note or a 2-step form.
- **Brand message** and a 30-second pitch.
- **Site at name.employz.ai:**
  - a "Chat on WhatsApp" button that opens the owner's own WhatsApp with "Hi, I saw your website…" typed;
  - an inquiry form feeding the leads inbox, with an owner alert.
- **Business AI Score out of 100:** a website check plus 10 questions, the top 3 fixes, and a shareable card.
- **Launch kit in brand colours:** WhatsApp Status poster, digital visiting card with QR, printable QR stand.
- **AI Writer** (was AI Studio):
  - a week of posts;
  - reply to a customer, then tap to send;
  - ask your business;
  - English polisher: Hindi/Hinglish text or a voice note becomes a polished English email, letter or WhatsApp.

  It opens with an intro card and has a worked example on each tool.
- **Growth loop:** a "Built free" badge, invite link and referral rewards. 10 AI runs a month.

## Membership — ₹1,999/month: a weekly rhythm

- **Monday — "Your week is ready":** 7 posts plus 3 actions, taken from the Magic Number and the Gaps scan.
- **Daily:** today's post plus one 5-minute action. Ticking it off builds a streak.
- **Friday:** a 2-minute check-in (leads, sales, what got done) that gives a weekly score.
- **Monthly:**
  - a month of posts, including Indian festivals and occasions;
  - a Business Score re-check against last month;
  - the member call, with a question box and the recording added to Recordings.
- **Cohort board:** streaks and "most consistent this week" within the workshop batch. Owners can opt out.
- **Ready-made tools:**
  - Content Agent and video scripts;
  - Google profile posts and review replies;
  - proposal writer and case study writer;
  - full English polisher;
  - SOP writer;
  - Magic Number planner and Gaps scan.
- **No badge** on the site.
- **Funnel and dashboard.**
- **Removed:** GPT bots, the Wheel review and the old goal tracker.

## Action Program — ₹10,000: the program done with their data

- **Lead Magnet Builder:** publishes the guide at name.employz.ai/guide with a download form that captures leads.
- **Offer Ladder** (entry / core / premium), shown as a section on their site.
- **Goals in three types** (financial, functional, learning) with weekly check-ins.
- **Revenue Levers:** customers × value × frequency, the 5-stage funnel, and the 10×10×10 pledge.
- **Customer and sales tools:**
  - Customer Portfolio (A/B/C);
  - Persona Builder;
  - sales role map (door openers vs closers);
  - Football Field pipeline (5 stages).
- **Funnel Leak Finder**, telecalling script, 30-day marketing plan, and the 4 end goals.
- **Also included:**
  - follow-ups and review requests (both tap-to-send);
  - decision log, SOP from a voice note, hiring kit;
  - morning digest to the owner.

## Running the Business — ₹25,000 (placeholder until the program exists)

- **Employz.ai CRM starter:** an Employz.ai account with the Football Field pipeline, and every hub lead synced into it. No WhatsApp, AI chat or domain.
- **Cash desk:**
  - money owed;
  - payment reminders prepared every morning, tap-to-send;
  - Quotation Officer (voice note → GST quotation);
  - invoice drafts;
  - quote follow-ups.
- **Customer desk:** referral requests, reorder reminders, quiet-customer alerts, occasion greetings. All tap-to-send.

## Growth Mentorship — ₹1,50,000, 6 months: "Set up on Employz.ai, with 6 AI staff"

**The 6 AI staff.** Month 1 is the setup itself.

| AI staff | What they do | Goes live |
|---|---|---|
| Sales Executive | Replies to every inquiry on WhatsApp 24×7, qualifies the lead, books the meeting, follows up quotations | Month 2 |
| Telecaller | ElevenLabs voice: calls new leads within minutes and re-calls old ones; call summaries go into Employz.ai. Only calls people who enquired or are customers | Month 3 |
| Marketing Executive | A month of posts and reel scripts, scheduled via Employz.ai; case studies | Month 4 |
| Accounts Executive | Quotations, invoices and payment reminders on WhatsApp | Month 5 |
| Customer Care Executive | Answers customer questions; asks for reviews and referrals; reorder reminders | Month 5 |
| Chief of Staff | Morning digest, pipeline analysis, meeting actions sent to the team on WhatsApp, monthly review | Month 6 |

**Done for them by the team:**
- Employz.ai CRM, pipeline, team users and calendar;
- their own domain, with a multi-page website;
- their own WhatsApp number on AiSensy;
- Company Brain: price list, catalogue, FAQs and past quotations;
- their old customer list imported, cleaned and sorted into A/B/C.

**Bonuses:**
- a monthly money campaign: old-customer revival, a festival offer or a referral drive. The owner approves it once; messages go only to past contacts, using Meta-approved templates;
- Control Room, plus a Monday CEO report on WhatsApp;
- Sales Training Gym: an AI role-play customer who haggles, and every call scored;
- a monthly AI results report;
- team: 10 logins with the PACE tools.

**Approval rules:** replies to customers who wrote first are automatic; anything the business starts waits for approval.

## LegacyWorkforce — ₹6,00,000, 1 year: a full AI workforce

- **30 AI staff:** 6 in each of 5 departments (Marketing, Sales, Customer Support, Business Intelligence, Operations & People), plus the Chief of Staff. The owner picks them from the 82-role catalogue and can swap them any time.
- **The office:** the owner types one instruction and the Chief of Staff plans it across departments.
- **Acting alone:** the owner can let staff act on their own. Messages with links always wait for approval.
- **Connections:** Employz.ai, WhatsApp and ElevenLabs, plus Tally, email and calendar.
- **Team logins:** 25.
